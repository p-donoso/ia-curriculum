# LinkedIn output model

The current LinkedIn view presents the saved professional summary, projects, and skills. A complete editable LinkedIn draft remains future work. Output should be an editorial adaptation of confirmed career records, with AI suggestions clearly marked and reviewed before publication.

## Planned sections

- Introduction: name, headline, location, industry, photo, and contact information.
- About: mission, motivation, strengths, and value proposition.
- Experience: roles, organizations, dates, context, achievements, and related skills.
- Education: institutions, programs, dates, and descriptions.
- Skills: relevant capabilities grouped and supported by evidence.
- Featured: projects, publications, links, documents, and work samples.

Possible extensions include certifications, courses, recommendations, volunteering, publications, awards, languages, organizations, patents, and test results. These are planning items, not claims about implemented features.

References: [Add sections to your profile](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a540837/adding-sections-to-your-profile?lang=en) and [Your LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a564064/your-linkedin-profile-overview?lang=en).
